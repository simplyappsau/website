Simply Invoice

A simple, low-fuss invoicing app for Australian sole traders and small businesses. Create a GST-ready invoice in seconds, send it to your client, and see at a glance what has been paid and what is still outstanding. When your accountant needs a record, export everything in one go.

How it works

  1. Create. Add your client, the work you did and the amount. Simply Invoice lays it out as a clear, professional invoice, including GST where it applies.
  2. Send. Email the invoice, or share a link your client can open in a browser, so they know exactly what to pay and by when.
  3. Track. Mark invoices as paid or unpaid and see outstanding amounts without keeping a separate spreadsheet.
  4. Export. Download a clean record of your invoices for your accountant, or for your own books at BAS or tax time.

Who it is for

Simply Invoice is for people who bill clients in Australia and want a straightforward way to get paid: trades, consultants, freelancers and other small operators who do not need a full accounting package just to send an invoice. It is designed to sit alongside tools such as Simply Receipts, so keeping records and billing clients can stay simple.
