Simply Receipts

The simple receipt keeper for Australian businesses and sole traders. Snap a receipt, and Simply Receipts reads the total and merchant, names the file and files it in the right month. When it’s time for your accountant, share the whole month with one link.

How it works

  1. Snap. Open the app and it goes straight to the camera. Take a photo of your receipt, or upload one on the web.
  2. Detected automatically. Simply Receipts reads the total and the merchant from the photo for you.
  3. Saved and named. Each receipt is saved straight away with a clear, numbered name such as 0001-$42.50-Bunnings, in a folder for that month.
  4. Shared with your accountant. Send a secure web link to a month’s folder. Your accountant can view and download receipts in a browser, with no app or account needed.

Features

  • Camera-first captureThe camera is the first screen, so recording a receipt takes seconds.
  • Automatic total and merchantNo typing: the key details are picked up from the photo.
  • Auto-saved, auto-namedFiles are numbered in order each month and named with the amount and merchant.
  • Monthly foldersReceipts are organised by month, ready for your BAS or tax time.
  • Accountant share linksShare a month by web link. Links can be revoked at any time and expire automatically.
  • Web sign-in and bulk downloadSign in on the web with the same account to download one receipt, a month, or everything.
  • Multiple companiesKeep receipts for several businesses separately in one account.
  • Australian-hostedReceipt photos and account data are stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • Originals kept safeThe original photo of each receipt is kept as your record.
