Simply Receipts

The simple receipt keeper for Australian businesses and sole traders. Snap a receipt, and Simply Receipts reads the date, total and supplier, names the file and files it in the right month. When it is time for your accountant, share a month, a selection or an ongoing link.

Built to take the filing off your hands

What it does for you automatically

Snap a receipt. Simply Receipts reads it, names it and files it, so you are not typing details or sorting folders at the end of the month.

  1. Snap. The app opens on the camera. Photograph a receipt on your phone, or upload one on the web.
  2. Detect. It reads the date, the total amount and the supplier from the photo. You can edit any of these if you need to.
  3. Name. Each file is renamed with a number for that month, the amount and the supplier, for example 3 - $107.23 Medco Petroleum.jpg.
  4. File. The receipt goes into the right company’s folder for the receipt date. You can keep more than one company in the same account.
  5. Share. Send a web link for a month, a selection of receipts, or an ongoing accountant link. Your accountant can view receipts in a browser — no app or account needed — and download them one at a time, as a ZIP of selected months, or as a ZIP of everything in the share. Month and selection links expire after 30 days. An ongoing accountant link stays active until you revoke or replace it.

Features

  • Camera-first captureThe camera is the first screen, so recording a receipt takes seconds. On the web, you can also upload a photo.
  • Date, total and supplier, read for youThese details are taken from the photo, and you can edit them if something needs correcting.
  • Auto-named filesEach receipt is numbered in order for that month and named with the amount and supplier, for example 3 - $107.23 Medco Petroleum.jpg.
  • Filed by company and monthReceipts are organised into the right company’s folder for the receipt date, ready for BAS or tax time.
  • Accountant share linksShare a month, a selection or an ongoing accountant link. Your accountant can view and download receipts in a browser. Month and selection links expire after 30 days; you can revoke any link at any time.
  • Downloads when you need themDownload one receipt, a ZIP of selected months, or everything, from your account or from a share link.
  • Multiple companiesKeep receipts for more than one company separately in the same account.
  • Australian-hostedReceipt photos and account data are stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • Originals kept safeThe original photo of each receipt is kept as your record.
