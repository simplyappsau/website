Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

This policy explains how Simply Apps, a business name of QA Advisers Pty Ltd (ABN 58 674 800 518) (“we”, “us”), handles personal information when you use the Simply Receipts app and its website, simplyapps.com.au.

If you have questions, email privacy@simplyapps.com.au.

Information we collect

  • Account email. To create and sign in to your account, send essential service messages (for example about your account or purchases), and reply when you contact us.
  • Company names. To keep receipts for each company you add separate.
  • Receipt photos. To store a copy of each receipt you capture or upload, and to read the details below. Photos are compressed on your device before upload to save space, while keeping them clear and readable.
  • Details read from receipts. Totals, merchant names and dates, so Simply Receipts can name and file your receipts and show and share your records.
  • Usage counts. How many receipts you have saved, so we can apply your 10 free receipts and any upload packs you buy.
  • Basic technical data. Limited information such as device type, operating system version and error logs, only to run, secure and fix the app.

We collect only what we need for these purposes. If you contact us, we also keep your message and email address so we can respond.

How we use information

We use this information to provide Simply Receipts: accounts, receipt storage, automatic naming and filing, share links you create, upload packs, support, security and reliability. We do not use your receipts to advertise to you, Simply Receipts shows no third-party ads, and we do not sell personal information.

Where your data is stored and processed

We use these service providers, which process information only to provide their services to us:

  • Supabase for sign-in, database and file storage. Your account data and receipt photos are stored in the Sydney, Australia region (Amazon Web Services ap-southeast-2).
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) Textract to read the total, merchant and date from receipt photos. This processing also happens in the Sydney region (ap-southeast-2).
  • Payments. If you buy upload packs, payment is handled by the Apple App Store, Google Play or our payment provider. We do not receive or store your full card details.

Share links you create

You can create a web link that lets someone you choose, such as your accountant, view and download receipts for one company and month. They do not need an account. Anyone with an active link can see those receipts, so share links only with people you trust. You can revoke a link at any time, and links expire automatically.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep your account, receipt photos and receipt details while your account is open so you can use your records. Purchase records may be kept as required for tax and accounting. If you close your account or ask us to delete it, we will delete or de-identify personal information we no longer need, unless the law requires us to keep it.

To delete your account, email privacy@simplyapps.com.au from the address on your account, or use the in-app deletion option once available. We will confirm when it is done.

Children

Simply Receipts is for business record keeping and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information, including access controls, encryption in transit and storage in Australia. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and complaints

We aim to handle personal information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). You can ask to access or correct your personal information by emailing privacy@simplyapps.com.au.

If you have a complaint, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date above.
