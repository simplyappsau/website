RK

RK is a receipt keeper for Australian small business owners and sole traders. Photograph a receipt, and RK detects the total and the merchant automatically. There is no subscription — it is pay as you go. Store links will appear here when RK is available.

What it does

  • Snap, detect, saved Point the camera at a receipt. RK reads the payment total and the merchant name, then saves the receipt automatically — no extra tap once the total is found.
  • Named for you Files are named in a consistent pattern, such as 0001-$42.50-Bunnings, and filed into monthly folders based on the receipt date.
  • Several companies One account can hold more than one company, each with its own receipts.
  • Bulk download Download your saved receipts together when you need a pack of records.
  • Share with your accountant Create a share link for a folder, so your accountant can view those receipts without needing their own RK account.
  • Pay as you go Use what you need. There is no subscription in version one.

RK is coming soon on iPhone, Android and the web. It is not in the App Store or on Google Play yet, so there are no download links on this page.
