Questions

What is RK?

RK is a receipt keeper. You photograph a receipt, and RK detects the total and merchant automatically, then saves and names the file for you in a monthly folder. You can keep more than one company, download receipts in bulk, and share a folder with your accountant.

When can I download it?

RK is not yet in the App Store or on Google Play. This site will be updated when it is available. There are no store links at the moment.

How much does it cost?

Version one is pay as you go. There is no subscription. Prices will be shown in the app when RK launches.

Where is my data stored?

Account and receipt files are stored in Sydney (Amazon Web Services region ap-southeast-2) using Supabase. Receipt text is extracted with AWS Textract in the same region. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

When the app is live, email privacy@simplyapps.com.au from the address on your account, or use any in-app deletion option if one is provided. We will confirm when the account and associated personal information have been deleted, or explain if something must be kept for a legal reason.
