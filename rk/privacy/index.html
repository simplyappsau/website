Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Effective date: 16 November 2026.

This policy describes how Simply Apps, a business name of QA Advisers Pty Ltd (ABN 58 674 800 518) (“we”, “us”), handles personal information when you use the RK app and related websites.

RK is a receipt-keeping app. It is not directed at children. If you have questions, email privacy@simplyapps.com.au.

Information we collect

We collect the following information when you create an account or use RK:

  • Account email. Used to create and sign in to your account, to send essential service messages (for example about your account or billing), and to respond when you contact us.
  • Company names. Used to organise receipts under each company you add to your account, so each business keeps its own records.
  • Receipt photos. Used to store a copy of each receipt you capture or upload, and to extract the details described below. The original image is kept so you have a record of the document itself.
  • Extracted totals, merchants and dates. Taken from your receipt photos so RK can name files, file them into monthly folders, show your records, and let you search and share them.
  • Usage counts for billing. We count saved receipts (and any other billable use we describe in the app) so we can charge on a pay-as-you-go basis and show you what you have used.
  • Basic device and diagnostic data, if collected. We may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, and crash or error logs, only to operate, secure and fix the app. If this collection is not used in a given release, this section still describes the types of data that may be collected.

We do not ask you to provide more personal information than we need for these purposes. If you contact support, we will also have the content of that message and the address you use to write to us.

How we use this information

We use the information above to provide RK, including accounts, receipt storage, automatic naming and filing, sharing that you turn on, pay-as-you-go billing, customer support, security, and improvement of reliability. We do not use your receipt photos or extracted receipt details to serve you advertisements, and we do not sell your personal information.

Service providers (processors)

We use specialist providers to run RK. They process information only to provide their services to us:

  • Supabase. Authentication, database and file storage. Receipt photos and account data are stored in the Sydney region (ap-southeast-2).
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) Textract. Text extraction from receipt images, in the Asia Pacific (Sydney) region (ap-southeast-2).

These providers may also process limited technical information needed to deliver their service. We do not sell data to them, and we do not allow them to use your receipt information to advertise to you.

Advertising and sale of data

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your information for third-party advertising, and RK does not show third-party ads.

Share links that you create

You can create a share link so someone you choose (for example your accountant) can view selected receipts or a folder. You control when a link is created and when it is revoked. Anyone who has the active link can view the shared records until you revoke it. The recipient does not need an RK account. You should only share links with people you trust, and revoke a link when it is no longer needed.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account, receipt photos and extracted details while your account is open, so you can use your records. Billing records of usage may be kept as required for accounting and tax. If you close your account or ask us to delete it, we will delete or de-identify personal information we no longer need, except where we must retain it for a legal obligation (for example tax or dispute records) or to resolve a complaint.

To request deletion of your RK account and associated personal information, email privacy@simplyapps.com.au from the address on the account, or use any in-app account-deletion control if one is provided. We will confirm the request and tell you when deletion has been completed, or explain if some information must be kept for a legal reason.

Children

RK is made for business record-keeping and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact privacy@simplyapps.com.au and we will take steps to delete it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information, including access controls, encryption of data in transit, and storing receipt files in the Sydney cloud region described above. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Australian Privacy Principles and complaints

We handle personal information in a manner intended to be consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). You may request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you by emailing privacy@simplyapps.com.au.

If you have a privacy complaint, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC). See oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints.

Contact

Privacy questions, access or correction requests, and account-deletion requests: privacy@simplyapps.com.au.
