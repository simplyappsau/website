About us

About Simply Apps

Simply Apps is an independent app studio based in Pymble, on Sydney’s Upper North Shore. We make practical, low-fuss apps for Australian people and small businesses.

Our story

Simply Apps grew out of everyday frustrations in running a small business: shoeboxes of fading receipts, end-of-month scrambles for the accountant, and software that charges a monthly fee whether you use it or not. Our motto, “Life is an adventure. Stay foolish, stay curious,” is a reminder to keep asking whether there is a simpler way to do things, and then build it.

Our first app, Simply Receipts, turns a photo of a receipt into a tidy, named record in a monthly folder that can be shared with an accountant in one link. It is coming soon to iOS, Android and the web.

Simply Invoice is next: a low-fuss invoicing app for Australian sole traders and small businesses, so you can create and send GST-ready invoices, see what is paid and unpaid, and export a record for your accountant. It is also coming soon to iOS, Android and the web.

What we value

  • Simplicity. Apps that do one thing well and get out of your way.
  • Honesty. Clear pricing, plain-English policies and no dark patterns.
  • Privacy. We collect only what an app needs, host data in Australia where we store it, and never sell personal information.
  • Curiosity. We keep learning from the people who use our apps and improve them over time.
