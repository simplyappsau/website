Frequently asked questions

What is Simply Receipts?

Simply Receipts is a receipt keeper. You photograph a receipt, and Simply Receipts detects the total and merchant automatically, then saves and names the file for you (for example 0001-$42.50-Bunnings) in a monthly folder. You can keep more than one company, download receipts in bulk on the web, and share a month with your accountant. More about the app.

When can I download it?

Simply Receipts is coming soon to the App Store, Google Play and the web. It is not available to download yet, and this site will be updated when it launches.

How much does it cost?

Each new account gets its first 10 receipts free. After that it is pay as you go: you buy upload packs when you need them. There is no subscription. Pack sizes and prices will be shown in the app at launch.

How does my accountant see my receipts?

You create a share link for a company and month and send it to your accountant. They open it in any web browser to view or download receipts, or download the whole month as a ZIP file. They do not need the app or an account. You can revoke a link at any time, and links expire automatically.

Can I use it for more than one business?

Yes. One account can hold several companies, and each company has its own monthly folders and share links.

Where is my data stored?

Account data and receipt photos are stored in Sydney, Australia (Amazon Web Services region ap-southeast-2) using Supabase. Receipt text is read with Amazon Textract in the same Sydney region. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

Email privacy@simplyapps.com.au from the address on your account, or use the in-app deletion option once it is available. We will confirm when your account and associated personal information have been deleted, or explain if something must be kept for a legal reason.
